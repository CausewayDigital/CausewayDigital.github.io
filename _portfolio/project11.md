Join a medical adventure in Detect & Diagnose!
Get hands on with the amazing equipment that doctors & specialists use every day to track down cancer.
- Track down tumours with CT scans
- Explore the lungs to take a sample of a tumour
- Prepare the sample & analyse it under a microscope.  

Have you got what it takes to become a Cancer Detective?
   
Working with The Ruth Strauss Foundation, leading researchers in the impact of parental illness and bereavement on children (Dr Olly Clabburn and Dr Rachel Fearnley) and clinicians from the UK NHS (National Health Service), Causeway Digital produced the first medical piece of content for Minecraft Education. The world features a full hospital build, equipped with many design features from NHS hospitals across the UK. It distills the complex process of cancer diagnosis into a 30-40 min series of activities for students aged 8-11.   
The world has been played in over 170 countries across the world by children and adults alike.    
   
Detect & Diagnose is available on both [Minecraft Education](https://education.minecraft.net/en-us/lessons/detect-diagnose) and [Minecraft Marketplace](https://www.minecraft.net/en-us/marketplace/pdp/causeway-digital/detect--diagnose/3ac4c0de-4180-45fd-885f-f1c7c11ecf52) for free.
